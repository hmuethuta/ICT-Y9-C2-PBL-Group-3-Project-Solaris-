About Project Solaris

Project Solaris is an interactive educational website focused on heatwaves, extreme temperatures and their effects on people and communities.

Heatwaves are becoming an increasingly serious concern in many parts of the world. They can affect people's health, damage crops, create water shortages and make everyday activities more difficult. However, many people may not fully understand the risks or know how to protect themselves.

Through this project, we aim to make information about heatwaves easier to understand and more accessible. Our website provides explanations, practical safety tips and useful information about staying safe during periods of extreme heat.

What we aim to achieve

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide clear, reliable and accessible information about heatwaves. We aim to help people understand the causes and effects of extreme heat, recognise potential dangers and learn practical ways to protect themselves and others.

Our Vision

We envision a future where people are more knowledgeable and prepared for extreme heat. We hope to contribute to communities where heat safety is widely understood and fewer people suffer from preventable heat-related illnesses.
