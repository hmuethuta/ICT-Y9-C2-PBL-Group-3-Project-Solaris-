Extreme Heat: Extreme Danger

Extreme heat events are becoming more frequent and intense due to climate change, posing serious risks to public health. Project Solaris aims to provide information and resources to help people prepare for and respond to these events effectively.

Learn more Staying safe

Learn about heatwaves

Who is most at risk?

Heatwaves can affect anyone, but older adults, young children, outdoor workers and people with existing health conditions are especially vulnerable. People living in cities may also experience higher temperatures because roads and buildings absorb and retain heat, even after the sun goes down.
